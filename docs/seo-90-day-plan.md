# SEO 90-Day Plan (On-site Focus)

This roadmap is built for execution with a split of responsibilities:

- **Our side (Sprint execution):** Technical SEO + on-site content + internal linking + programmatic SEO
- **Site owner side:** Backlinks and external authority building

## Targets and KPIs

Measure weekly in GSC + analytics dashboards:

- **Impressions:** +60% in 90 days (baseline week 0)
- **Clicks:** +45% in 90 days
- **CTR:** +0.8 to +1.5 percentage points on priority pages
- **Top 3 keywords:** 25+ non-brand high-intent queries in top 3
- **Organic leads:** +30% lead actions from organic sessions (calls, WhatsApp, form submissions)

## 90-Day Timeline (Weekly)

## Weeks 1-2 (Sprint 1 - Foundation + Quick Wins)

- Fix metadata, canonical, schema coverage, sitemap/robots consistency
- Strengthen internal linking on homepage, cars, city, brand, and blog templates
- Launch first programmatic SEO starter pages (city + brand + intent)
- Add execution roadmap and tracking checklist

## Weeks 3-4 (Sprint 2 - Expansion)

- Expand city + brand + intent pages based on inventory and query demand
- Add unique supporting copy blocks for top cities/brands
- Improve blog clustering and contextual links to conversion pages
- Add SEO QA checks for indexability and duplicate risk

## Weeks 5-8 (Sprint 3 - Scaling)

- Scale programmatic pages in controlled batches (index quality first)
- Introduce richer schema coverage for list/detail templates
- Optimize underperforming pages via CTR-focused title/description iterations
- Build content hubs for high-value intents (monthly, luxury, airport, tourist use-cases)

## Weeks 9-12 (Sprint 4 - Performance + Consolidation)

- Prune/merge thin pages and improve pages near positions 4-10
- Add stronger conversion modules for organic landing pages
- Tighten crawl budget control (priority URLs, parameter handling)
- Final 90-day review: KPI delta, winners/losers, next-quarter roadmap

- `/car-rental/abu-dhabi/kia/monthly`
- `/car-rental/sharjah/hyundai/daily`
- `/car-rental/sharjah/nissan/monthly`
- `/car-rental/damascus/toyota/daily`
- `/car-rental/damascus/hyundai/monthly`
- `/car-rental/aleppo/kia/daily`

Selection rule:

- Start with cities/brands that have enough live listings
- Keep intent templates limited and useful (`daily`, `monthly`, `luxury`)
- Ensure each page has: real inventory, unique intro copy, FAQs, and strong internal links
